Research

Research focus #

My research focuses on Multimodal Large Language Models, with a current focus on SpeechLLMs, models that process spoken language. As a postdoctoral researcher at FBK, I have been working within the European projects Meetween, DVPS, and LLMs4EU. My PhD research was partially supported by the TANGO European Project, and during my research internship at Telefónica R&D in Barcelona I worked under the SPATIAL European Project on private and fair federated learning.

European projects #

These are European projects I have contributed to at different stages of my research; they are not projects I led or won.

Current contributions #

  • Meetween: a Horizon Europe project developing multilingual meeting AI, including speech-to-speech translation, summarisation, and multimodal speech models. I am contributing to deliverables and to the fine-tuning of models.
  • DVPS: a Horizon Europe project on multimodal foundation models combining speech, text, video, gestures, and other modalities. I am contributing to the development of Personalized Multimodal LLMs.
  • LLMs4EU: an EU project focused on adapting and deploying language models across European languages and application areas.

Previous contributions #

  • TANGO: a Horizon Europe project on hybrid human–machine decision making. My PhD research was partially supported by this project.
  • SPATIAL — a Horizon 2020 project on secure, privacy-preserving, and trustworthy AI. During my research internship at Telefónica R&D in Barcelona, I worked on private and fair federated learning under SPATIAL.
