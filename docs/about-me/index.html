Hi there I'm Luca 👋

This is a short introduction of myself. If you want a more detailed description you can read my CV (longer version) or visit my LinkedIn account

Hi 👋, I am Luca, a postdoctoral researcher at Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK) in Trento 🇮🇹, where I work in the Machine Translation Unit. My current research focuses on Multimodal Large Language Models, with a special interest in Speech.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the University of Pisa 🇮🇹 in July 2025, with the highest grade (summa cum laude), under the supervision of Prof. Anna Monreale and Dr. Nicolas Kourtellis. During my PhD I worked on AI safety for Federated Learning: privacy, fairness and explainability. I have authored papers at venues such as FAccT and ECAI (see my research), spent a year as a research intern at Telefónica Research in Barcelona 🇪🇸, and collaborated with Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz.

Outside the lab I am a speaker, a community builder (Superhero Valley and Pisa.dev) and a podcaster (310+ episodes).

 Software Projects #

  • PUFFLE: a regularization-based method that balances privacy, utility and fairness in Federated Learning, with custom fairness requirements defined by practitioners (Mar 2023 – ongoing).
  • Fire360: a fast local explainer for tabular data that uses a single synthetic neighborhood instead of costly per-instance generation (Feb 2025 – Mar 2025).
  • FastShap++: an extension of FastSHAP to Federated Learning with Differential Privacy to protect client data during explanation (Oct 2024 – Feb 2025).

Podcast and Communities #

🎙 I have always been attracted to communities.

Between 2013 and 2015, I worked as an editor for Biteyourapple.net, writing more than 1,000 blog-posts, recording videos and participating in the Bitecast podcast. I am one of the PointerPodcast co-founders. PointerPodcast is a podcast about technology and innovation, published since 2019. In some episodes, we interview software engineers and researchers from academia and industry. I have also co-founded two communities. The first is Superhero Valley, which fills the gap between university and industry by making students aware of the opportunities offered by big tech companies. The second is Pisa.dev, a local community that organizes meetups for developers and computer scientists in Pisa 🇮🇹.

🍦 In my free time I enjoy acting as a critic of TV shows, mainly in my podcast.
